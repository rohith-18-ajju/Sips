Gold and silver: a beginner's guide

Precious metals are often used to balance a portfolio, because they can behave differently from shares. They also pay no interest or dividends, so your gain depends only on the price rising.

Ways to invest

Gold ETFUnits trade on the stock exchange and track the gold price. Needs a demat account.Small yearly fee and brokerage.
Gold fund of fundsA mutual fund that invests in gold ETFs. Works with a SIP and needs no demat account.Slightly higher fees than an ETF.
Silver ETFSame idea as a gold ETF, but for silver.Silver prices swing more than gold.

Digital goldBuying small amounts of gold online through an app.These products are generally not regulated like mutual funds. Check the provider carefully.

Simple rules of thumb

Many planners suggest keeping gold to a small slice of your total investments, often quoted as 5 to 10 percent. Treat this as a starting point, not a rule, and decide based on your goals and risk comfort.

For students, a monthly SIP into a gold fund or ETF is usually simpler and cheaper than buying jewellery as an investment.
